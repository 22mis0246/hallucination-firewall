BANKING KNOWLEDGE BASE - UPI

UPI

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) enables customers to make digital payments and transfers using supported UPI applications.

UPI transactions require authentication according to the applicable payment method and bank or UPI service requirements.

Transaction limits can vary depending on the bank, account, transaction type, and applicable rules.

Customers should check their bank or payment application for the applicable transaction limit.

UPI Security

Customers should never share their UPI PIN with another person.

A UPI PIN is used to authorize certain transactions.

Customers should verify the recipient and transaction amount before approving a payment.

Customers should report unauthorized transactions to their bank or the appropriate payment service as soon as possible.
