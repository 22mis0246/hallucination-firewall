BANKING KNOWLEDGE BASE - ACCOUNTS

Savings Account

A savings account allows customers to deposit money and earn interest on eligible balances.

Customers can deposit and withdraw money from their savings account through supported banking channels.

The bank may require customers to maintain a minimum balance depending on the type of savings account.

Interest on savings accounts is calculated according to the applicable interest rate and account terms.

Customers should check the applicable account terms and conditions for fees, minimum balance requirements, and interest rates.

Current Account

A current account is primarily designed for customers who need frequent transactions.

Current accounts may have different fees, transaction limits, and balance requirements from savings accounts.

Interest is generally not the primary feature of a current account.

Account Security

Customers should never share their PIN, password, OTP, CVV, or other authentication credentials with another person.

Banks will not require customers to disclose confidential authentication credentials through unsolicited messages or calls.
