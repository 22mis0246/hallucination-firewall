BANKING KNOWLEDGE BASE - CARDS

Debit Card

A debit card allows customers to make purchases or withdraw cash using funds available in the linked account, subject to applicable limits.

Customers should protect their card, PIN, and authentication information.

Credit Card

A credit card allows eligible customers to make purchases using a credit facility subject to the card's approved limit and terms.

Credit card transactions may be subject to interest, fees, and other applicable charges depending on the transaction and card terms.

Customers should make payments according to the billing statement and applicable due date.

Card Security

Customers should never share their PIN, CVV, OTP, password, or other confidential authentication information.

If a card is lost or stolen, the customer should contact the bank promptly and follow the bank's card-blocking procedure.
