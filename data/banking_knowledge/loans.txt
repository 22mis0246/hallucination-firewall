BANKING KNOWLEDGE BASE - LOANS

Personal Loan

A personal loan is a type of credit that allows an eligible customer to borrow money and repay it according to agreed terms.

Loan eligibility may depend on factors such as income, credit history, existing obligations, and the lender's internal policies.

The interest rate, loan amount, tenure, processing charges, and other terms depend on the specific loan product and customer eligibility.

Customers should review the loan agreement before accepting a loan.

Loan Repayment

A loan is generally repaid through scheduled installments according to the repayment schedule.

Missing or delaying repayments may result in applicable charges and may affect the customer's credit history.

Customers should contact the lender if they anticipate difficulty making a scheduled repayment.
